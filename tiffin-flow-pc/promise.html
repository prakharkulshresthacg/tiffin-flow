How It Works

Designed to be reused

One meal shouldn't mean one box in the trash. Here is how our eco-friendly architecture operates.

01

Reusable Stainless Steel Tiffins

Meals, not disposable trash. Our meals are delivered in durable, food-grade 304 stainless-steel tiffins engineered to be sanitized and reused thousands of times. Food stays hot naturally without leaching toxic microplastics or chemicals.

02

Zero Single-Use Plastic Policy

Skip unnecessary plastic. We eliminate disposable plastic lids, plastic forks, cling films, and plastic carrier bags. Meals travel in insulated washable thermal cotton sleeves that keep meals warm.

03

Nature-Friendly Materials

Thoughtful choices everywhere. From unbleached biodegradable paper napkins to compostable wooden cutlery provided only upon explicit customer request, we consciously minimize every trace of waste.

04

The Closed-Loop Tiffin Cycle

Deliver → Enjoy → Return → Steam-Sanitize → Reuse. Your dabba isn't meant for the dustbin. It enters a seamless neighborhood collection loop where it is medically sanitized at 120°C before the next meal.
