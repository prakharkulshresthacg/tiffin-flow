Self-Service Help

Common questions & answers

How do I change my delivery address if I move flats?
Go to your Profile or Contact Support. As long as your new address is within our operating cluster zones, your deliveries can be redirected with 24 hours notice.
What if I forget to leave the empty dabba outside?
No problem! You can hand it over on the subsequent delivery day. We maintain a float of two dabbas per customer to ensure you never miss a meal.
How does the ₹85 wallet refund work on skipped meals?
Any meal skipped before 10 PM the night prior automatically adds ₹85 to your TiffinFlow Wallet. When your plan cycle renews, the wallet balance is auto-deducted from your payable total.
