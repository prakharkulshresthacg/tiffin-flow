Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Got questions about how subscriptions, dabba deposits, or cancellations work?

Can I skip meals if my college or work schedule changes?
Yes! You can skip any upcoming lunch or dinner anytime before 10:00 PM the night prior. When you skip a meal, ₹85 is credited instantly to your TiffinFlow Wallet, which automatically deducts from your next billing cycle.
How does the reusable stainless-steel dabba deposit work?
We require a one-time refundable security deposit of ₹200 for your pair of food-grade stainless steel dabbas. When your delivery arrives, our delivery partner collects the clean, empty dabba from the previous day. If you cancel your subscription, the deposit is 100% refunded.
Can I pause my subscription when going home for holidays?
Absolutely! In the Standard Flex and Premium plans, you can pause your delivery calendar for up to 10 to 20 consecutive days. Your remaining meal balance remains frozen and resumes the day you return.
Can I change my dietary preference midway through the plan?
Yes, you can toggle between Pure Veg, Non-Veg, or Jain meal options for any specific day in your Smart Weekly Meal Planner with a single click.
Are there any hidden delivery or handling charges?
None at all. Delivery is 100% free across all our operating cluster zones in Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, and Pune.
